Common questions

How much does bridal makeup cost in Delhi? +

Most brides pay between ₹15,000 and ₹40,000 for HD makeup with a trial. Airbrush and premium artists run higher. See the pricing guide above for a fuller breakdown.

What's the difference between HD and airbrush makeup? +

HD makeup uses cream and powder products built for high-definition cameras. Airbrush is sprayed on for a lighter, more even finish that tends to hold up longer in heat and humidity.

How far in advance should I book? +

Good artists get booked up 4-6 months ahead during wedding season (October-February and April-June). Book as soon as your date is confirmed.

Do artists travel to the wedding venue? +

Most do, usually for an additional travel fee depending on distance. This is confirmed directly with the artist once you're matched.

Is a trial session included in the price? +

It depends on the package. Some artists include one trial in their bridal package; others charge separately. We'll flag this when you compare quotes.
